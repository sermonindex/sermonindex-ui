# Brand Guidelines

[[_TOC_]]

As per our Open Source Permissions page, we have a policy to allow for the free distribution of
sermon materials on SermonIndex. Our logo and branding can be copied also for referencing our site
as per below. As per our About Us page, our mission statement is:

> SermonIndex’s assignment is to honour and preserve the past preaching of God’s Word and to promote
> revival to this generation.

Our short form bio for the ministry is: _Founded in 2002. Access over 100,000+ sermon resources from
classic preachers like: A.W. Tozer, Leonard Ravenhill, C.H. Spurgeon, etc._

Our official hashtag is: `#sermonindex`

## Colour

We have two main colours that have and do represent the SermonIndex ministry, it is suggested to
utilize these in any materials, magazines or other ways that have incorporated our logo.

```
HEX: #595935
HEX: #B2A364
```

## Logo

Our Primary logo (dark green with white background). Where possible this is the best way to display
our logo.

You can use our white overlay logo on any overlay situation that requires it to go over images, we
utilize this method on the SermonIndex image quotes that we post on social media.

When referring to our ministry we ask you to use: SermonIndex or SermonIndex.net (with
capitalization). Example: "Download free resources from SermonIndex.net" or "These sermons are used
by permission of SermonIndex.net" etc.
