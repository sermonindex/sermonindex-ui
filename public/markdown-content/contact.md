# Contact

We are here to serve and help you however we can with the sermons on the site. Please first review
the FAQs below and if there is not a resolution to your problem you can contact us at the email
address at the bottom of the page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q. How Do you download the audio/video sermons?

> **Answer:** On each sermon page we have a clear section labeled **download** where you can click on a link to download the mp3 file of the sermon. If it is an video sermon there will be an mp4 file download (for text sermons a PDF download will only appear). You can also now download the PDF or TXT of all audio and video media!

#### Q. Can I copy these sermons to my website or burn them to CDs?

> **Answer:** Yes. All materials on SermonIndex.net can be freely copied for personal use, distribution, and re-sharing. We encourage that the materials be distributed as widely as possible only many mediums such as CDs, USB sticks, radio, other websites, etc. You can read more about this on our [Copying Permissions page](/md/copying-permissions).

#### Q. Can I put a link to SermonIndex.net?

> **Answer:** Yes. This is encouraged and it helps us spread these messages to many more people. You can find SermonIndex logos and more details on our [Brand Guidelines](/md/brand-guidelines) page.

#### Q. Some speakers on SermonIndex vary in their doctrinal stance?

> **Answer:** The sermons have been chosen from an very wide selection of different denominations across Christendom. We have added some sermons and books for historical value and studying. We believe that with humility no matter what our interpretation of Scripture we can learn from each other and grow in Christ-likeness. You can read a doctrinal statement of belief on our [About page](/md/about). We agree with the 18th century moravian Count Zinzendorf in his below quotes:

:::card-start

 ![Count Zinzendorf](https://sermonindex3.b-cdn.net/pdf/countzinzendorf.png)

 :::card-middle

> "Religion is known by the heart, rather than the head… It consists of a living relationship with Christ. This can be described in different ways and expressed in different practices in different Christian churches, but the reality behind all descriptions is relationship with God.” and “I ask not whether he be a Lutheran, or a Calvinist, or an Anabaptist. If he believes in the Savior, and loves Him in truth and sincerity, I call him brother.”

:::card-end



#### Q. How can I submit my sermons or obtain hosting for my sermons?

> **Answer:** All sermons are carefully curated and chosen by prayer. SermonIndex does not host sermons for churches or ministries as a service. All materials have been hand-chosen and selected to be hosted for free. You are welcome to submit and mail sermons in but over 80% of the materials submitted are not accepted for addition to Sermonindex. We intend to focus on adding older vintage, rare audio sermons from the past century as well as historical text messages from Church history. If you feel you have a message or resource you want to share you can mail or email us below.

#### Q. Can I financially support this work?

> **Answer:** Yes. The website of SermonIndex is 100% supported by the generous community of supporters who pray and have been blessed by the messages. All messages are offered freely and part of how they are made available in this way is through monthly supporters that help with all the costs of the website and ministry. You can learn how to [support SermonIndex here](/md/support).
