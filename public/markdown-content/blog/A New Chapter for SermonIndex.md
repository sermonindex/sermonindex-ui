# A New Chapter for SermonIndex.net — Our Website Has Been Renewed

After much prayer, planning, and work behind the scenes, we are grateful to announce the launch of the new [SermonIndex.net](https://www.sermonindex.net) website.

For over two decades, SermonIndex has been a place where believers from all over the world can access historic sermons, revival resources, and messages that lift up the name of Jesus Christ. Our heart has never changed — to encourage a deeper walk with God, to inspire personal holiness, and to point the Church back to the simplicity and power of the Gospel.

## The new website has been redesigned to:

Serve You Better: A clean, mobile-friendly design makes it easier to read, listen, and share.

Search Faster: Improved navigation and search tools help you find messages and articles quickly.

Access More Content: The full archive of audio sermons, transcripts, and articles remains available — and continues to grow.

Prepare for the Future: Built on a faster, more stable platform to reach more people around the globe.

Our prayer is that this new design will not only make SermonIndex more accessible, but also more effective in equipping God’s people for the days ahead.

## New Online Bible Content

With this relaunch, we are excited to introduce major new Bible study resources:

30+ Open-Source English Bible Translations — Read and compare Scripture across multiple faithful translations.

Bible Helps & Study Content — Cross-references, topical studies, and verse-by-verse insights to deepen your understanding.

By-Verse Bible Pages — Explore the Scriptures one verse at a time, with linked resources for richer study.

Plans for Even More — We’re preparing to add trusted Bible commentaries, historical notes, and additional study tools in the months ahead. Our prayer is that this new platform will not only make our resources more accessible, but will also equip you with the tools to study God’s Word more deeply and to walk more closely with our Lord.

## Our Open Source & Copying Policy

From its beginning, SermonIndex has existed to freely distribute the Word of God and the preaching of Christ without charge — in the words of Jesus, “Give as freely as you have received” (Matthew 10:8). Following the example of Paul the Apostle, we seek to present the Gospel “without charge” (1 Corinthians 9:18), and stand in the historic tradition of ministers like C.H. Spurgeon, George Fox, Augustine, and Cyprian who all warned against selling the Gospel.

We have submitted the entire SermonIndex website, databases, code and content (whereas applicable) into the public domain CC0 1.0 Universal The rights holder has waived all copyright so that it may be used without hindrance.

If otherwise noted at the bottom of this page you can:

Copy — Share and distribute the resource in whole or in part
Modify — Improve or adapt the resource
Translate — Make the resource available in a new language

Soon, we will also provide ways to download our sermons, Bibles, and resources in bulk, ensuring that the Scriptures and their faithful exposition remain free digitally to the public for generations to come.
